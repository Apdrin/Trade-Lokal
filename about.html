About TradeLokal

Connecting local communities with trusted, handcrafted, and sustainable products.

Our Mission

TradeLokal supports local businesses by showcasing authentic Filipino goods and making them easier to find, browse, and support online.

We believe every purchase should strengthen communities, promote sustainability, and celebrate local craftsmanship.

Community First

Every seller is a local entrepreneur, artisan, or farmer.

Eco-Friendly

We promote products made with sustainable materials and mindful practices.

Growth & Impact

Local business exposure means more jobs, better income, and stronger neighborhoods.

Why Choose TradeLokal?

We offer a curated experience built around trust, product quality, and meaningful local impact.

  • Handpicked sellers with real local stories
  • Transparent pricing and product details
  • Easy browsing across food, crafts, clothing, and agri goods
